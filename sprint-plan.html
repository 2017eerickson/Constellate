Constellate MVP Sprint Plan

Sep 10, 2026 - Dec 12, 2026 | 7 Sprints (2 weeks each)

Sprint Process (Do This Every Sprint)

  • Daily standup (5 min): Ask yourself — What did I do yesterday? What's today? What's blocking me?
  • Sprint planning (Day 1, 30 min): Review upcoming tasks, adjust based on what you learned last sprint
  • Sprint review (Last day, 30 min): Demo what you built, note what's working and what isn't
  • Retrospective (Last day, 15 min): What went well? What slowed me down? What will I change next sprint?
  • Velocity tracking: Count completed tasks per sprint — if you're consistently finishing <80%, reduce scope
  • Definition of Done: A task is done when it works locally, has been manually tested, and has no console errors
Size Estimates: S = few hours M = half day to full day L = 1-2 days
